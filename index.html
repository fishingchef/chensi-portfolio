About

Technical Program Manager &
V&V Engineer

Technical Program Manager and V&V Engineer with 7+ years of experience in regulated medical device environments — spanning full product program delivery, large-scale physiological data collection operations, and formal software verification and validation.

At BioIntelliSense, I led iOS/Android mobile applications and the BioHub Wi-Fi gateway from concept through commercial launch while simultaneously owning large-scale data collection programs end-to-end — recruiting 300+ participants, setting up ground truth devices (Faros ECG monitors, Capnostream capnography), authoring protocols, managing ICF and consent workflows, collecting multimodal physiological signals (step/gait, HR, RR, temperature, audio), and owning the full data labeling, annotation, and cleaning pipeline with internal data scientists and Lofty Consulting (Australia).

As V&V Engineer, I led software V&V across firmware, mobile, and cloud platforms — supporting three FDA 510(k) submissions under IEC 62304 and ISO 13485, and authoring 1,000-cycle cleaning and disinfection validation protocols for wearable device reprocessing.

I'm also a hands-on AI builder — built and shipped Fish Smarter using Claude (Anthropic) and Gemini AI APIs, and built this portfolio site with Claude's help. I use Claude daily as a productivity and problem-solving tool.
